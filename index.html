Txtify ("we", "our", "us", or "the app"), developed and operated by Vineyard Tech, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Txtify accesses, processes, stores, and safeguards information when you download, install, and use the Txtify mobile application.

1. Information We Handle and How It Is Processed

  • Selected Files, ZIP Archives & Converted Text Files (100% On-Device Processing): Txtify allows you to select individual files, batch files, folders, or .zip archives on your device and convert their contents into plain .txt files saved in your chosen output directory. All file reading, ZIP extraction, filtering, and .txt creation happens strictly locally on your device. Vineyard Tech does not upload, view, collect, or store your source files, ZIP archives, file names, folder structures, or converted text files on any Vineyard Tech server.
  • Local Preferences & Saved Folder Paths: Txtify stores only your chosen destination save folder URI (saveFolderUri) and optional in-app file browser root folder URI (browserRootFolderUri) locally inside Android SharedPreferences (TxtifyPrefs) so you do not have to re-select your folders every time you open the app.
  • Local Crash Diagnostic Logs: If Txtify experiences an unexpected crash, an on-device handler records a local crash report in your app's private external files directory (crash_reports/). These reports contain the crash timestamp, stack trace, and basic device hardware/OS details (device brand, model, and Android OS version). Crash reports remain strictly on your device and are not automatically uploaded anywhere.
  • Support Inquiries (In-App Contact Form & Email): When you voluntarily submit a message through the in-app Contact screen or email us directly, the details you provide—specifically your name, email address, and message—are transmitted via HTTPS to our form processing provider (Formspree) and delivered to our support inbox solely so we can respond to your inquiry or bug report.

2. Device Permissions & File Access

  • Storage Access Framework (User-Selected Files & Folders): Txtify uses Android's standard Storage Access Framework (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT and ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE) with persistable read/write URI grants only for the specific files, ZIP archives, and folders you explicitly choose. Txtify does not scan or access unrelated files on your device without your selection.
  • Network & Internet Access: Used strictly to load Google AdMob advertisements, check for Google Play In-App Updates, and submit support messages if you choose to use the in-app Contact Us form.
  • No Account Required: Txtify does not require registration, login, or user accounts, and does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Txtify integrates the following third-party services, which may process limited device, diagnostic, or support submission data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Displays banner advertisements and app open advertisements within Txtify. AdMob may automatically collect and process your Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID, IP address and general approximate location (country/city level), app and ad interactions (such as launches, taps, and ad views), and performance diagnostics (such as launch time and crash/hang metrics).
  • Google Play In-App Updates (Play Core): Connects with Google Play Services on launch and resume to check whether a required application update is available so you remain on a secure, supported version of Txtify.
  • Formspree (In-App Contact Form Processing): When you submit the optional Contact Us form inside Txtify, your entered name, email address, and message are securely sent over HTTPS to Formspree (formspree.io) for delivery to Vineyard Tech's support team.

For more information on how Google and Formspree handle data, please review:
Google Privacy Policy · Google Advertising Technologies · Formspree Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention, User Controls & Deletion Requests

Because Txtify does not create user accounts or maintain a cloud database of your files, most data resides solely on your own device and is under your immediate control.

How to Request Deletion of Support Correspondence

  1. Submit a Deletion Request by Email: Send an email to contact@vineyardtech.online or josymvine@gmail.com with the subject line Txtify Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include Identifying Details: Send the request from (or mention) the email address you used when contacting us via the in-app Contact Us form or email so we can locate and purge your support messages.

Processing Timeframe: All verified deletion requests for support messages and contact form submissions are completed within 30 days of receipt, with confirmation sent to your email.

In-App & Device-Level Controls

  • Clear Queued Projects & Folder Permissions: You can clear queued projects anytime inside Txtify via the top menu ("Clear All"), switch your save or browser folders at any time, or clear app storage in Android Settings to wipe TxtifyPrefs and revoke persisted folder URI grants.
  • Delete Local Crash Reports or Uninstall App: Uninstalling Txtify immediately deletes TxtifyPrefs and the app's private crash_reports/ folder. (Note: .txt files already saved into your chosen device folder remain in your storage until you delete them using your file manager.)
  • Third-Party Ad Telemetry Controls: Advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob are governed by Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Summary of Data Retained

  • Files & ZIP Contents: Never collected or retained by Vineyard Tech (processed 100% locally on your device).
  • Support Inquiries: Emails and Contact Us form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your support request (up to 90 days if inactive), or permanently deleted within 30 days upon request.

5. Security of Your Information

Txtify performs all file reading, ZIP inspection, and text conversion locally on your device. All external network communications—including Google AdMob, Google Play In-App Updates, and Contact Us form submissions—are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or files to any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

Txtify is a general file-conversion utility and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal contact details through our support form, please contact us so we can promptly delete that information.
